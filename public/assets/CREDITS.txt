Species heights (decimeters) and weights (hectograms) copied from PokéAPI's source data:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/master/data/v2/csv/pokemon.csv
REST access returned HTTP 403 in the development environment; the project's public CSV source was used instead.
Data snapshot: src/species.json. Rendering height = 32 * sqrt(height_m / 0.4).
