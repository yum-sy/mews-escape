## Forest prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide 2:1 background plate for an animated game opening, no characters. Primary request: a secluded sunlit forest clearing before a tragedy. Style: accomplished hand-inked storybook animation background, expressive charcoal contour, delicate crosshatching, textured watercolor washes, restrained cinematic lighting, slightly ominous but peaceful. Scene: ancient crooked tree trunks frame the left and right, layered fern foliage and roots in the foreground, a soft open clearing in the center, receding misty trees and pale diagonal afternoon sun shafts. Composition: a low flat clearing occupies lower quarter, middle 45 percent is quiet negative space for a separately composited small floating character. Palette: desaturated moss green, warm cream sunlight, deep blue-green shadows. Painted production background, not flat geometric shapes or vector icons. No humans, animals, Pokemon, creatures, characters, text, lettering, interface, logos, watermark, borders. Landscape wide composition.

## Laboratory prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide 2:1 background plate for a dark animated game laboratory, no characters. Primary request: an abandoned underground biological research laboratory just after containment failure. Style: accomplished hand-inked storybook animation background, expressive charcoal contour, delicate crosshatching, textured watercolor washes, cinematic chiaroscuro. Scene: damaged cabinets and inactive monitors at left and right, suspended cables, cracked wall panels, scattered paper and small broken glass on a perspective floor, dusty overhead lamps, a narrow pale exit doorway far right. Composition: center 40 percent is an empty open staging area for a separately animated containment chamber and characters; do not draw a chamber, capsule or creature in the center. Vanishing point center at upper-middle, floor bottom third. Palette: cool slate blue-grey, deep charcoal shadows, tiny amber emergency indicators. Rich material texture and coherent depth, not flat rectangles or vector UI. No humans, animals, Pokemon, creatures, bodies, gore, text, lettering, interface, logos, watermark, borders. Landscape wide composition.
